## Description

Robert Skvarla, Jr. ([`/people/robert-skvarla`]) filed this request under the Freedom of Information Act ([`/documents/freedom-of-information-act`]) through MuckRock ([`/organizations/muckrock`]) with the Department of Defense ([`/organizations/dod`]), Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security ([`/organizations/ousd-is`]). It requests copies of records for a definitive contract (award ID: HQ003422C0064) awarded to Sancorp Consulting, LLC ([`/organizations/sancorp-consulting`]) for support services in the Influence and Perception Management Office ([`/organizations/ipmo`]), including, but not limited to, correspondence, statements of work, award letters, invoices and presentations by Sancorp Consulting, LLC. The letter points to the May 17, 2023 report by Ken Klippenstein ([`/people/ken-klippenstein`]) of The Intercept ([`/organizations/the-intercept`]) on the IPMO, requests a fee waiver as a member of the news media, and says Sancorp already lists the contract on their publicly-accessible website.

The Freedom of Information Division ([`/organizations/dod-freedom-of-information-division`]), which handles FOIA requests for the Office of the Secretary of Defense ([`/organizations/osd`]) and the Joint Staff ([`/organizations/joint-staff`]), assigned case number 23-F-0906. Its interim response of May 24, 2023, signed by Stephanie L. Carr ([`/people/stephanie-carr`]), Chief, cited unusual circumstances, placed the request in the complex processing queue and named Tyra Howard ([`/people/tyra-howard`]) as the Action Officer. The agency correspondence gives the request date as May 21, 2023, while the MuckRock page gives Submitted May 19, 2023.

The agency FOIA log ([`/documents/whs-foid-foia-log-fy23`]) records the request as received via PAL on 2023-05-22 and closed on 2023-07-28 with the Final Disposition Granted / Denied in Part; the MuckRock page for the same request shows Status Awaiting Response. The MuckRock page counts 7 Communications, but the archived page shows four of them, and the correspondence here does not include the final response. The Washington Headquarters Services ([`/organizations/whs`]) Contracts page lists files under 23-F-0906: the Final RFQ ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-final-rfq`]), the NDA-NPI ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-nda-npi`]), the DD 254 ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-dd254`]), the Award Notification ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-award-notification`]) and Contract ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-contract`]) for HQ0034-22-C-0064, and the Performance Work Statement ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0906-sancorp-ipmo-pws`]).
