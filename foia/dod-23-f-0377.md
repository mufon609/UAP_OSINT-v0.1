## Description

FOIA case 23-F-0377 was filed by John Greenewald ([`/people/john-greenewald`]) of The Black Vault ([`/organizations/the-black-vault`]). The FOID ([`/organizations/dod-freedom-of-information-division`]) FOIA log ([`/documents/whs-foid-foia-log-fy23`]) of the DoD ([`/organizations/dod`]) records the request as received via PAL on 2023-01-19 and closed on 2023-06-23, with the Final Disposition Granted / Denied in Part.

In the request text the log records, the requester seeks records pertaining to an October 19, 2021 meeting ([`/events/2021-10-19-dod-oig-tipton-meeting`]) between the DoD/OIG ([`/organizations/dod-inspector-general`]) and Neill Tipton ([`/people/neill-tipton`]), OUSD, as revealed in FOIA case DODOIG-2023-000021 ([`/foia/dodoig-2023-000021`]). The request text names Tipton's office only as OUSD; neither source states which Under Secretary office it is. The requester states the meeting was regarding the Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding the Unidentified Aerial Phenomena ([`/documents/dod-oig-evaluation-dod-actions-regarding-uap`]) and that the email confirming this meeting took place ([`/documents/foia-dodoig-2023-000021-oig-email-20211104`]) was sent on November 4, 2021. The request covers additional emails, including emails internal at the OSD/JS ([`/organizations/osd`], [`/organizations/joint-staff`]), material utilized during the meeting, and memos, and it seeks a search for all classified and unclassified records. These are the requester's words, not an agency finding.

The Black Vault timeline entry names Tipton as Director for Defense Intelligence, Collection and Special Programs ([`/organizations/ousd-is-ddi-csp`]). It states that OSD/JS FOIA Case 23-F-0377 further confirmed the meeting with the release of the actual calendar details in the files of Mr. Neill Tipton ([`/documents/foia-23-f-0377-tipton-calendar-20211019`]). That is the requester's account of the release. The log records the disposition but does not list the records released.
