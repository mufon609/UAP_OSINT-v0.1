## Description

The Defense Intelligence Agency ([`/organizations/dia`]) Congressional Relations Division letter U-18-0001/OCC1, dated JAN 09 2018, over the signature of Christine Kapnisi ([`/people/christine-kapnisi`]), Chief, to The Honorable John McCain ([`/people/john-mccain`]), Chairman, and The Honorable Jack Reed ([`/people/jack-reed`]), Ranking Member, of the Committee on Armed Services, United States Senate. Based on interest from the Committee's staff regarding DIA's role in the Advanced Aerospace Threat and Identification Program (AATIP), the letter states: "please find attached a list of all products produced under the AATIP contract for DIA to publish." The purpose of AATIP was to investigate foreign advanced aerospace weapon threats from the present out to the next 40 years.

There are 38 reports associated with DIA's involvement in the program documented in the list. All are UNCLASSIFIED//FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY except for attachment 37, which is a SECRET//NOFORN version of attachment 38, titled "State of the Art and Evolution of High Energy Laser Weapons." All are available for Congressional staff access on the Capitol Network (CapNet) except for attachments 12 and 15, respectively titled "Field Effects on Biological Tissues" and "An Introduction to the Statistical Drake Equation." The Enclosure "(U) List of Attachments" documented, under each attachment number, the product and the Dr. who produced it — among them Dr. George Miley ([`/people/george-miley`]), Univ. of Illinois (attachment 1, "Inertial Electrostatic Confinement Fusion" [`/documents/dird-09-iec-fusion`]); Dr. F.
