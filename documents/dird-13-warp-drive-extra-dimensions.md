## Description

Warp Drive, Dark Energy, and the Manipulation of Extra Dimensions. The Defense Intelligence Reference Document is 2 April 2010, with an ICOD of 1 December 2009 and control number DIA-08-1004-001, UNCLASSIFIED//FOR OFFICIAL USE ONLY. Where the Prepared by and Authors appear, the document shows (b)(3):10 USC 424 and Defense Intelligence Agency ([`/organizations/dia`]) and, on the Authors line, (b)(6). An Administrative Note states the product is one in a series of advanced technology reports produced in FY 2009 under the Defense Intelligence Agency, Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications (AAWSA) Program [`/organizations/aawsap`], and that comments or questions should be addressed to the AAWSA Program Manager. The document is one of a series of FY 2009 AAWSA Program advanced technology reports ([`/documents/dia-aatip-products-list-2018`]); the authors ([`/people/r-obousy`], [`/organizations/obousy-consultants`]) appear there, not on the document, which shows only (b)(6) on the Authors line.

The Introduction states that physicists have discovered two loopholes to Einstein's ([`/people/albert-einstein`]) ultimate speed limit — the Einstein-Rosen bridge (commonly referred to as a wormhole) and the warp drive — and that both involve manipulation of spacetime itself in some exotic way that allows for faster-than-light (FTL) travel. The warp drive, the main focus of the paper, is defined as local manipulation of the fabric of space in the immediate vicinity of a spacecraft via an asymmetric bubble of space that is contracting in front of the spacecraft while expanding behind it; the spacecraft remains stationary inside this warp bubble and the movement of space itself allows the relative motion of the spacecraft. The paper states that its own research introduces a novel paradigm shift in the field of warp drive study by approaches the physics of warp drive from the perspective of quantum field theory rather than the more traditional general relativity, and that one of the improvements the model introduces is a dramatic reduction in the overall energy required to create such a phenomenon. Dark energy is given as an exotic and ubiquitous form of energy believed to constitute over 70 percent of the matter-energy content of the universe, with the intrinsic ability to generate negative pressure that causes the fabric of space to expand. The paper's research focus is on gaining an understanding of the physical origin of dark energy through ideas at the forefront of theoretical physics, incorporating cutting-edge ideas from string theory and quantum field theory, and to consider a mechanism that would allow a sufficiently advanced technology to control the dark energy density in any region of space and thus the expansion of space — work with clear implications for the advancement of warp drive research.

Section 2 reviews general relativity warp drive requirements: implementation of FTL interstellar travel via warp drives requires engineering of spacetime into very specialized local geometries, and the analysis requires the use of exotic matter, defined in general relativity as matter that possesses (renormalized) negative energy density and/or negative stress-tension; it has been claimed that since exotic matter violates the energy conditions FTL spacetimes are not plausible, but this has been shown to be a spurious issue (Reference 11 = Davis ([`/people/eric-davis`]) 2009). Equation (2.3) and Table 2 show that the warp bubble energy requirement is an enormous negative number even at arbitrarily low sublight speeds, and Equation (2.4) shows that for the example starship parameters (R = 50 m, ship mass 10⁶ kg) v_warp falls so low that garden snails can crawl faster than this. Section 3 introduces the cosmological constant Λ as an inherent energy density associated with empty space, with the late 1990s discovery that the rate of expansion of the universe is increasing, it has become more popular to refer to Λ as dark energy. Section 4 states that the origin of the Casimir effect cannot be traced back to one of the four fundamental forces but is a force entirely due to a modification of the quantum vacuum; even though it is purely quantum in nature it manifests itself macroscopically (for two parallel plates of area A = 1 cm² separated by 1 μm the force of attraction F ≈ 1.3 x 10 ⁷ N), a basis that the paper takes as motivated to relate the ubiquitous dark energy field to this quantum vacuum energy. Section 5 reviews Kaluza-Klein theory, the Arkani-Hamed ([`/people/nima-arkani-hamed`]), Dimopoulos ([`/people/savas-dimopoulos`]) and Dvali ([`/people/gia-dvali`]) (ADD) large extra dimensions model (in which setting the effective Λ equal to the known density of dark energy the extra-dimensional radius is r ∼ 10⁻³ cm), and the Randall ([`/people/lisa-randall`]) Sundrum ([`/people/raman-sundrum`]) brane models (whose warp factor and bulk Casimir energy can provide a mechanism for the generation of dark energy on the visible brane). Section 6 presents the paper's own work: through a novel regularization of the extra-dimensional vacuum potential, it is possible to tune the theoretical energy density of the universe to agree with experimental observations once contributions due to the extra-dimensional quantum vacuum fields are included, and once one knows why space expands, it becomes possible to explore technological possibilities to potentially make space expand. Section 7 presents the warp drive proposal on the relation that the energy is related to the radius of the higher dimension raised to the fourth power: an advanced technology able to influence the radius of an extra dimension would acquire direct control over dark energy and hence the expansion and contraction of space itself, stated as one of the only viable mechanisms to generate a warp drive at this early stage in the research; the key to creating a warp drive is to create a false vacuum minimum, to modify the vacuum spectrum and create a deSitter minimum at the rear of the craft and an anti-deSitter minimum at the front, to create the warp bubble previously discussed. Table 3 — the dimensional warp drive paradigm — shows energy requirements reduced by a factor of 10⁸ compared to the calculations of Visser ([`/people/matt-visser`]) and Lobo ([`/people/francisco-lobo`]) for a 100 m³ warp bubble, with possible further reduction via the thin shell model, though the paper states that even with this vast improvement the energies are still far in excess of those available in the foreseeable future. Section 7.5 proposes a table-top dark energy laser experiment using petawatt laser technology that, if it could output an exotic field with w < −1, would translate into a measurable local expansion of space along the length of the laser; Section 7.6 states the technological developments necessary to allow prototype experiments to begin — a more complete understanding of dark energy and attempts to generate dark energy in the lab, and determining whether extra dimensions are in fact real —, and the Atlas experiment at CERN's Large Hadron Collider as the experimental tool with the capability to probe ADD type extra dimensions up to M_D ∼ 8 TeV. Section 8 states that the idea that a sufficiently advanced technology may interact with, and acquire direct control over, the higher dimensions is a tantalizing possibility, and one that is most certainly worthy of deeper investigation.

### Central claim of Section 6 — by including extra-dimensional vacuum-field contributions and allowing exotic fields in the higher dimension, the theoretical vacuum energy density can be tuned to match observed Λ. The "taming" of the cosmological-constant problem central to the paper's argument.

### Engineering recipe stated explicitly: induce a deSitter minimum aft and an anti-deSitter minimum forward of the craft via false-vacuum-minimum technology to produce the asymmetric warp bubble.
