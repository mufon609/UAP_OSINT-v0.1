Data Analyst / Technical Program Consultant III

Position Overview

Premier Enterprise Solutions is seeking a highly skilled Data Analyst / Technical Program Consultant III to support a federal government customer in the areas of performance assessment, strategic analysis, data analytics, and decision support. The selected candidate will leverage advanced analytical techniques, data visualization tools, and strategic planning methodologies to evaluate organizational effectiveness, assess mission outcomes, and support senior leadership decision-making.

Location

Washington, DC Metropolitan Area (onsite)

Security Clearance

Active TS/SCI Clearance Required

Required Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Data Analytics, Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field.

Experience

Minimum seven (7) years of experience in data analytics, data visualization, statistical analysis, mission analysis, or performance assessment. Experience supporting federal government, DoD, intelligence, or national security organizations preferred.

Key Responsibilities

Conduct quantitative and qualitative performance assessments across defense intelligence and security enterprises.
Develop performance reports, strategic assessments, decisional papers, action memoranda, and executive briefings.
Develop and maintain organizational performance measures and data visualizations.
Conduct mission analysis, risk assessments, and performance improvement analyses.
Develop methodologies for organizational and program performance analysis.
Provide strategic analysis using multi-source data to support decision-making and budgeting cycles.
Develop strategy implementation guidance aligned with national and defense priorities.
Track and assess strategy implementation, compliance, and performance.

Technical Skills

Advanced data visualization and dashboard development
Statistical analysis and predictive analytics
Python, R, VBA, Java, or similar tools
Machine Learning (ML) and Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Executive-level reporting and briefing development
