 Company Summary Arlo Solutions (Arlo) is an information technology consulting services company that specializes in delivering technology solutions. Position Description: The Cybersecurity Portfolio Manager oversees a coordinated group of security programs, projects, and engagements — managing them collectively rather than in isolation so Arlo invests in the right work, at the right time, for the right return. Applying PMI-aligned portfolio and program management practices, this person defines portfolio components, establishes prioritization criteria, allocates resources, manages interdependencies and risk, and reports portfolio value to executive stakeholders. The role blends rigorous delivery oversight with a forward-looking, growth-oriented posture demonstrating portfolio thinking and the adaptability to shift between tactical coordination and strategic alignment. Equally important is the customer-facing dimension: the Portfolio Manager serves as a senior point of contact for clients, surfacing and resolving issues quickly, shaping new business opportunities, and championing Arlo’s decision support threat intelligence tool as a force multiplier for client risk decisions. Location: Arlo Solutions HQ / Hybrid (client-site travel as required) Clearance: TS/SCI Preferred Responsibilities and/or Success Factors: Portfolio Management & Governance Define, balance, and authorize the cybersecurity portfolio—aligning components to organizational strategy and ensuring the optimal mix of programs and projects. Establish and apply portfolio prioritization, selection, and value-measurement criteria, making recommendations on which initiatives to start, continue, reprioritize, or terminate. Manage portfolio-level risk, interdependencies, resource capacity, and the portfolio roadmap, maintaining balance across scope, schedule, cost, and benefit. Govern portfolio performance against defined KPIs and benefit-realization targets; lead portfolio reviews and report status, risks, and value to executive leadership. Apply PMI standards (Standard for Portfolio Management, PMBOK) and Arlo governance frameworks to ensure consistent, auditable, and transparent decision-making. Program Delivery & Execution Oversight Coordinate the successful delivery of cybersecurity engagements and initiatives, collaborating with project leads, technical SMEs, and delivery teams. Translate strategic goals into outcome-based program plans and milestone tracking. Monitor program progress, identify risks, and recommend adjustments to ensure on-time, on-budget, and high-impact delivery. Cross-Team Integration & Governance Collaborate closely with senior managers across development, design, and delivery teams to ensure consistent planning, agile coordination, and cohesive delivery. Support agile ceremonies and team coordination, enabling transparent workflows and shared accountability. Maintain project and portfolio management systems and ensure up-to-date records of risks, actions, and decisions. Customer-Facing Delivery & Issue Resolution Serve as a senior, trusted point of contact for clients across the portfolio, building durable relationships at the practitioner and executive levels. Translate complex cybersecurity findings and threat information into clear, actionable guidance that supports client decision-making. Team Coordination & Leadership Coordinate cross-functional delivery teams, program managers, and subject-matter experts to execute portfolio components effectively.
