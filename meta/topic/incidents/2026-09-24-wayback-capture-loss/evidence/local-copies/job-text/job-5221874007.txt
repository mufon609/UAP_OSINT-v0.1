Position Description: The Logistics Analyst II provides logistics support to U.S. Marine Corps operations with a focus on DoDAAC/DoDAAD management, logistics data analysis, process improvement, tasker management, training, policy support, and customer service. The successful candidate will combine logistics knowledge with strong critical-thinking, analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills to research issues, identify root causes, develop practical solutions, and communicate recommendations to government and organizational stakeholders. The position will utilize GCSS-MC, ELMs, WebFLIS, PIEE, ETMS2, Power BI, and Microsoft Office to manage logistics information, analyze data, track actions, develop reports, and support Marine Corps logistics operations. Work Location: Quantico, VA (Hybrid/Telework 3 Days Per Week) Clearance: Active Public Trust Clearance Responsibilities and/or Success Factors: Key Responsibilities: Manage and maintain DoDAAC/DoDAAD records, including researching, validating, reconciling, and processing activations, modifications, transfers, and inactivations. Research complex logistics and DoDAAC issues, identify root causes, evaluate available information, and develop practical, sustainable solutions. Analyze logistics data to identify trends, discrepancies, process gaps, and opportunities to improve efficiency, data integrity, and customer support. Manage and track ETMS2 taskers, ensuring actions are accurately assigned, monitored, coordinated, and completed within established deadlines. Develop and maintain tasker trackers, status reports, dashboards, metrics, and leadership briefings. Utilize Power BI and Microsoft Excel to analyze logistics data and develop dashboards, reports, and visualizations that support decision-making. Develop, review, edit, and maintain SOPs, internal guidance, process documentation, job aids, and other logistics-related materials. Research changes to U.S. Marine Corps and DoD policies, regulations, instructions, and procedures and assist with updating organizational processes and guidance. Translate complex policies and requirements into clear, practical procedures for personnel at various organizational levels. Develop and deliver training and informational briefings, including presentations to large groups, on logistics processes, policies, systems, and organizational procedures. Research and resolve discrepancies involving DoDAACs, logistics records, inventory data, serial numbers, NIINs, NSNs, and supporting documentation. Coordinate with Marine Corps commands, supply personnel, program offices, DLA, property personnel, and other stakeholders to resolve logistics issues. Identify and recommend process improvements and workflow efficiencies that improve data integrity, operational effectiveness, and customer support. Prepare written products, presentations, reports, briefings, and other materials for government and organizational leadership. Manage multiple priorities and independently execute assigned projects and actions while meeting established deadlines.
