Position Description: The Senior Directorate Management Liaison serves as a primary coordination and communication interface between AFRL/RF Directorate leadership and internal and external organizations. The position provides senior-level executive operations, strategic planning, protocol, workflow, communications, and organizational coordination support to ensure Directorate activities and leadership priorities are effectively synchronized. Clearance: Active TS Office Location: Wright-Patterson Air Force Base Responsibilities and/or Success Factors: Serve as a primary interface between Directorate leadership and organizational units. Coordinate closely with Front Office Group leadership and the Executive Officer to synchronize Directorate activities. Manage and update AFRL corporate and Sensors Directorate calendars. Support preparation and distribution of Directorate communications, including the "Daily Distilled" bulletin. Lead coordination of Distinguished Visitor, Congressional Delegation, Scientific Advisory Board, staffer, and other senior-level visits. Develop comprehensive itineraries and coordinate protocol, meals, logistics, briefings, deliverables, and execution requirements. Coordinate with Division Chiefs and other senior stakeholders to obtain required materials and prepare leadership for engagements. Plan quarterly Director's Calls and support agenda development, presentations, correspondence, documentation, and scripts. Support review and editing of Monthly Activity Reports. Maintain organizational charts, mission briefings, executive content, and other Directorate-level information products. Oversee workflow/tasker management, including receipt, dissemination, tracking, suspense management, and closure. Coordinate OPR/OCR assignments with Directorate leadership. Monitor executive communications during leadership absences and coordinate complex domestic and international visits with the AFRL Commander's Action Group. Minimum Qualifications Including Certificates: Active Top Secret Clearance 7-10 years' Experience Extensive experience supporting senior/executive leadership within a DoD, Air Force, Government, or comparable complex organization. Demonstrated expertise in executive operations, strategic coordination, protocol, and organizational workflow management. Exceptional written and verbal communication skills. Ability to independently manage high-visibility activities involving senior Government, military, Congressional, and scientific stakeholders. Strong judgment, discretion, organization, and ability to perform effectively under competing priorities and short deadlines.
