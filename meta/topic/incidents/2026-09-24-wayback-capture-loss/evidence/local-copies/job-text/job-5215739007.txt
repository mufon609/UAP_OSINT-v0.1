Position Description: The Senior Business Analyst serves as a strategic liaison between the AFRL Sensors Directorate's Chief Scientist/Chief Engineer organization and scientific, engineering, business, and other cross-functional stakeholders. The position translates scientific and engineering priorities and technical roadmaps into actionable business strategies, investment plans, performance measures, and executive-level decision support. Clearance: Active TS Office Location: Wright-Patterson Air Force Base Responsibilities and/or Success Factors: Provide senior-level strategic planning and business analysis support to the Chief Scientist and Chief Engineer. Translate scientific and technical roadmaps into business strategies, tactical plans, investment priorities, and actionable initiatives. Conduct market research and competitive analysis to identify emerging scientific and technological opportunities and risks. Conduct cost-benefit analyses supporting R&D investment, resource allocation, and project prioritization decisions. Develop and implement KPIs for R&D and engineering projects covering budget, schedule, technical quality, and other performance dimensions. Develop executive reports, dashboards, visualizations, forecasts, and trend analyses. Perform variance analysis and forecasting to identify emerging risks and support proactive management decisions. Gather and document business, functional, and technical requirements from scientists, engineers, and other stakeholders. Assess workflows and processes, identify inefficiencies, and develop recommended improvements. Support implementation of new business systems and processes aligned with Chief Scientist and Chief Engineer strategic objectives. Serve as the communication bridge between scientists/engineers and non-technical stakeholders. Lead or facilitate meetings and workshops to establish consensus, resolve issues, and align stakeholders around program and organizational objectives. Translate highly complex scientific and engineering information into concise, decision-oriented information for diverse audiences. Minimum Qualifications Including Certificates: Active Top Secret Clearance 7-10 years' Experience Advanced experience in strategic planning, business analysis, R&D/program analysis, or engineering-oriented business operations. Demonstrated ability to advise and support senior scientific, engineering, or executive leadership. Strong understanding of R&D portfolio management, performance measurement, investment analysis, and resource prioritization. Advanced analytical, facilitation, communication, and executive briefing skills. Ability to independently structure ambiguous problems, integrate complex technical/business information, and develop executive-level recommendations.
