# Draft: WHS — HQ003426FE050 performance work statement

**State:** `ready`. Queue: [`meta/topic/foia-queue.md`](../foia-queue.md)
**Submit to:** the OSD/Joint Staff FOIA office, which processes Washington
Headquarters Services records, via FOIA.gov.

## Why

The FE050 services description, "ADMINISTRATIVE, ANALYTIC, SCIENTIFIC,
INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY AND SUBJECT MATTER EXPERTISE SUPPORT SERVICES", appears
verbatim in Sancorp requisition 1258, which supports "…All-Domain Anomaly
Resolution Office (AARO)". No federal record names AARO on FE050, so the link is
flagged as unconfirmed in the Sancorp, AARO and Arlo nodes. The call order's
PWS would settle it either way.
